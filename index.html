The Mission

The Through-Line

I've spent my career chasing one simple idea: let computers handle the tedium so people can focus on creativity, ideas, and progress. That's why Expensify immediately resonated with me — your mission aligns perfectly with what I've been building toward my entire professional life.

The Beginning

From Artist to Engineer

I didn't start as an engineer. I studied graphic design and spent my early career in print. But I quickly realized that static design wasn't enough — I wanted to build things that moved and did something.

At a catalog company, I taught myself AppleScript and automated a three-month production process down to two weeks. I literally coded myself out of a job — but it set my career in motion.

Entrepreneurship

Catalyst Workflow Solutions

I started a small automation business, building custom production systems and early content management tools. My custom CMS had over 75,000 downloads before I let it go — a great product I simply didn't know how to market at the time.

That lesson still drives me: never walk away from a good idea too soon.

Web Development

From Builder to Problem Solver

Over the next several years, I shifted from design automation to full-stack web development, building sites and apps for clients large and small.

My proudest technical moment came while working at a telecom company: I used a K-Nearest Neighbor algorithm to predict service availability after a third-party API proved too slow. The result? Online conversions doubled overnight.

That experience taught me what I love most — solving real-world problems through smart automation and creative engineering.

Enterprise Scale

From Developer to Architect

My CMS and web work opened the door to enterprise consulting, where I led major CMS rebuilds for federal and Fortune 500 clients.

Later, I joined Iconfinder, a Danish startup, where I helped scale ingestion pipelines to handle 10,000+ products per day, growing inventory from half a million to over six million assets.

Current Role

Capital One

Today, I'm at Capital One, building event-driven AWS systems that audit over 1.5 million IAM roles nightly to ensure compliance and security.

It's technically challenging work — and important — but most of what we build is invisible. I miss creating things that people see and feel.

Side Project

VectorIcons.net

That's why I started VectorIcons.net, a multi-vendor marketplace for vector icons and illustrations. It's built on Next.js and Express, deployed on AWS with a serverless image-processing pipeline I designed myself.

It's both a passion project and a living lab where I test new technologies, architectures, and ideas — all centered around making creative work faster and more enjoyable.
